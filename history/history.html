Switch to oldest first?

To list the oldest entries at the top, the complete viewing history is loaded once and sorted oldest → newest.

Export the complete history?

The complete viewing history is loaded once and written to a file. For a very large history this can take a moment and triggers many requests. Nothing is imported into Watcharr.

Format

Every entry is matched against TMDB through your Watcharr instance (TMDB ID, type, title, year) – these columns make the file easy to import into other services.
