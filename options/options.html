How it works

  1. Open Netflix, Amazon Prime Video or your Jellyfin server and start a movie or series.
  2. The add-on detects the title, season, and episode from the service's player or metadata API.
  3. It searches for the title via your Watcharr instance (TMDB ID) and adds it to your watchlist (WATCHING).
  4. Once a movie/episode has been watched to ≥ 90% it is marked as watched (FINISHED).

You can adjust the behavior at any time via the popup or this page.

Data processing happens locally and only with your own Watcharr instance. No data is transferred to third parties.
